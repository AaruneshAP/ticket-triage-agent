    • 14-Day Resolution SLA: Standard service level target for Home Assistant Core bugs. Elapsed time > 14 days is classified as an SLA breach.
    • 0.35 High-Risk Threshold: Issues with predicted not-planned probability p ≥ 0.35 are flagged for proactive maintainer attention.
    • Strict Sandbox Write Allow-List: All bot comments and labels are restricted strictly to designated sandbox repositories (ticket-triage-sandbox). home-assistant/core is strictly read-only.
